Tip: In Safari, tap the Share icon and choose “Add to Home Screen”. The app then opens like a normal app. Please always use the same way to open it – texts saved in Safari and in the home screen app are kept separately.

How it works

  1. Paste a text or open a PDF. Your teacher may also give you a text file to open.
  2. Tap a word you don't know. Tap another word in the same sentence to mark a whole phrase.
  3. Look it up in a dictionary or ask an AI. Save the meaning – the word stays marked.
  4. Open My words to see everything you marked and export it for Quizlet, Anki, Word or as a PDF.
