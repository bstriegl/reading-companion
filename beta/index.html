How it works

  1. Paste a text or open a PDF. Your teacher may also give you a link, a QR code or a text file.
  2. Tap a word you don't know. Tap another word in the same sentence to mark a whole phrase.
  3. The built-in dictionary shows German meanings: tap the one that fits the sentence. For phrases and names you can ask an AI or use an online dictionary. The word stays marked.
  4. Open My words to see everything you marked, practise it with flashcards or export it for Quizlet, Anki, Word or as a PDF.
